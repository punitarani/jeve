# Screenshot manifest

44 shots. Playwright, headless Chromium with `--use-angle=metal` (renderer: ANGLE Metal, Apple M3 Max) unless the name says swiftshader. Production build (`next build && next start`), a replayed world on its own database (`jeve_shots`), driven to each sim-time by `ops/audit/shots.sh` / `shots2.sh`; script is `ops/audit/screenshots.ts`.

FPS is the page's `requestAnimationFrame` rate over 1.5 s on this machine at the moment of the shot. It was 57-61 everywhere on the real GPU, including the 4x building zooms. It is *not* the draw rate under software GL (WEB-0003 caps drawing at 15).

Two names carry `-LIVE`: those were taken with a paced daemon ticking. The un-suffixed `01`/`02` were taken after the daemon had reached its horizon (my sequencing mistake), so they show the stalled landing; both are kept because both states are real.
